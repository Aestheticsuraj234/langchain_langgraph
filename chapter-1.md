## What is AI and LLM?

**AI (Artificial Intelligence)** = Computers doing tasks that need human-like intelligence  
(e.g. understanding language, recognizing images, making decisions)

**LLM (Large Language Model)** = A type of AI trained on lots of text  
→ Can read, write, summarize, translate, and chat

**How it works:**
1. You send a prompt (question/instruction)
2. LLM predicts the next words
3. You get a response

**Key idea for this course:**
- LLM = the brain
- LangChain/LangGraph = the harness that makes the brain useful in real apps
- Agent = Model + Harness
